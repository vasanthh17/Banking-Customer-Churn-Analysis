# 🏦 Banking Customer Churn Analysis

A portfolio project analyzing why banking customers churn and identifying segments that should receive retention attention.

## 🎯 Objective
Answer three business questions:
1. Who is churning?
2. What factors are associated with higher churn?
3. Which customers/segments should the bank prioritize for retention?

## 🧰 Tools
**Python | Pandas | NumPy | SQL (MySQL) | Power BI | Excel | GitHub**

## 🧹 Data Cleaning
- Duplicate customer detection/removal
- Missing-value treatment
- Text standardization
- Invalid age and credit-score validation
- Product-count validation
- Negative transaction correction
- Derived age, activity, churn and high-risk fields

## 📊 Key KPIs
- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate
- Average Balance
- Average Credit Score
- High-Risk Customers
- Churned Balance

## 📈 Dashboard
Three Power BI pages:
1. Executive Overview
2. Customer Drivers
3. Risk & Recommendations

## 💡 Business Outcome
The dashboard helps prioritize retention campaigns toward customer groups showing combinations of inactivity, low satisfaction, early tenure, low product engagement and other churn-associated characteristics.

> Synthetic portfolio dataset — no real customer information.
